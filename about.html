About Aixuno Scan

Last updated: November 2026

Aixuno Scan is a free online document scanner that runs entirely in your browser. It turns phone photos into crisp, professional scans with automatic edge detection, perspective correction, enhancement filters, and on-device OCR — and none of your files are ever uploaded to a server.

Why local-first

Scanned documents are often the most sensitive files people handle: contracts, IDs, medical records, lecture notes. We believe a scanner should never ask you to hand those over. That is why every step — detection, filtering, OCR, and PDF creation — happens on your device.

The Aixuno ecosystem

Aixuno Scan is part of the Aixuno tool family: a Markdown converter, Pixkit image tools, file compression, PDF utilities, AI background removal, old photo restoration, and video watermark removal. Each tool is free, requires no account, and processes your files locally whenever the task allows it.
