Scan quality

Automatic edge detection, perspective correction, and enhancement filters are best-effort algorithms. Results depend on lighting, camera quality, and document condition. Always review the output before relying on a scan.

OCR accuracy

On-device OCR is a powerful convenience, but it is not perfect. Recognized text may contain errors, especially for handwriting, low-contrast pages, or unusual fonts. Proofread OCR results before using them in legal, financial, or academic contexts.
