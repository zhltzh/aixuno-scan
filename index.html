Frequently asked questions

Is Aixuno Scan really free?

Yes. All features — including OCR and PDF export — are free, with no watermark and no sign-up.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. Edge detection, enhancement, OCR, and PDF creation run entirely in your browser. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

What image formats can I scan?

JPG, PNG, and WebP from any source — photos from your phone, screenshots, or files. You can also capture pages directly with your device camera.

Which languages does OCR support?

English and Simplified Chinese (with English mixed in) are available today, with more planned. Recognition runs on your device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — the layout is fully responsive and camera capture works on mobile browsers (HTTPS required, which any hosted site provides).

How is this different from CamScanner?

No cloud upload, no account, no watermark, and no subscription. The trade-off: advanced AI retouching (moiré removal, finger occlusion restoration) is not available — core scanning and OCR are.
